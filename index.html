Our Mission

Building Brilliant Minds

Mynd is a student-led nonprofit on a mission to democratize access to STEM education. We design and deliver engaging engineering experiences for students who don't always get the chance to explore these fields.

Founded by aspiring engineers who believe every student deserves to discover their potential, we partner with schools to deliver workshops, mentorship, and hands-on challenges — completely free of charge.

Our Story

Why We Started Mynd

As students who have experienced the transformative power of STEM firsthand, Aarohi and Nancy saw too many of their peers missing out — not because of lack of talent, but lack of access. Mynd was born out of that frustration and that hope: that every young person can discover they are capable of building, coding, and engineering a better world.

Grow Your Skills

Expand Your STEM Horizons

Whether you're just getting started or already deep in the code, here are the best ways to level up your engineering and STEM skills — for free.

🏆 Go to Hackathons

Hackathon

Find Local Hackathons

Hackathons are weekend-long events where you build something cool with a team. Search Devpost, MLH (Major League Hacking), or ask your school about local events. Many are free and beginner-friendly.

Hackathon

Why Hackathons Matter

You'll meet like-minded peers, build a real project in 24–48 hours, and learn more in a weekend than you might in a month. Win or lose — the experience is invaluable for your portfolio and résumé.

Hackathon

Tips to Prepare

Practice problem-solving, learn the basics of HTML/CSS or Python, and go with a friend. Don't wait until you feel "ready" — hackathons are for learning, not perfection.

💻 Learn to Code

Coding

Start with Python 🐍

Python is the most beginner-friendly and widely used language in engineering and AI. Start with freeCodeCamp, Khan Academy, or CS50 on edX — all completely free.

Coding

Build Websites with HTML & CSS

Understanding how the web works is a superpower. The Odin Project and freeCodeCamp's Web Dev track will take you from zero to building real websites.

Coding

Try Scratch First 🎨

If you're brand new to coding, Scratch by MIT is the perfect visual starting point. Create games and animations while learning the logic of programming — no prior experience needed.

Coding

Java & JavaScript

Once you're comfortable with basics, Java is used everywhere in engineering and enterprise software. JavaScript is essential for web apps. Both have huge communities and free tutorials online.

Coding

Join a Coding Community

Sites like Replit, GitHub, and Stack Overflow connect you with millions of coders. Share projects, ask questions, and learn from others. Community accelerates everything.

Coding

Build Projects, Not Just Courses

The fastest way to learn is to build something real. Even a simple to-do list app or personal portfolio website teaches you more than hours of tutorials. Start small and keep shipping.

📚 More STEM Resources

Science

Science Olympiad & Competitions

Join your school's Science Olympiad team, enter robotics competitions like FIRST Robotics, or participate in math contests like AMC. Competitions build skills and look great on applications.

Engineering

Watch Engineering Content

YouTube channels like Mark Rober, Veritasium, SmarterEveryDay, and Real Engineering make complex engineering concepts fascinating and accessible.

Research

Find Research Opportunities

Many universities offer summer research programs for high school students. Look for REU programs, local university partnerships, or reach out directly to professors whose work interests you.
